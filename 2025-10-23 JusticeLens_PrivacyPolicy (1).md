# JusticeLens Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 2025_

## 1. Introduction

JusticeLens is an AI-powered legal education and research assistant created and maintained by **Michael Lyons**. JusticeLens is designed to help individuals, families, and legal clinics identify potential sentencing and conviction issues, using publicly available information and educational analysis. 

JusticeLens is **not a law firm**, does **not offer legal advice**, and should never be relied upon as a substitute for consulting a qualified attorney.

This Privacy Policy explains how JusticeLens handles information that users provide through OpenAI’s ChatGPT platform and any public API calls (such as CourtListener or Free Law Project).

For privacy-related inquiries, please contact:

📧 **michael.lyons@yimpactventures.com**

---

## 2. Data Collection and Use

JusticeLens itself does **not collect, store, or sell personal data**. All interactions occur within OpenAI’s ChatGPT platform, and any data shared is governed by **OpenAI’s Privacy Policy**.

When users upload case files or other documents for analysis:
- The files are **processed temporarily** to generate insights during the chat session.  
- No personal information is stored by JusticeLens outside the ChatGPT environment.  
- Data is deleted automatically when the chat ends unless the user explicitly saves or downloads outputs.

If you use any **Actions** (such as those powered by CourtListener / Free Law Project), only **publicly available legal data** is retrieved. No personal or confidential case data is ever sent to those services.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

JusticeLens uses public APIs and resources to enhance its legal research functions. These include:

- **CourtListener / Free Law Project** – for public case law and RECAP docket lookups  
- **govinfo.gov** – for U.S. Code access  
- **Official state statute websites** – for state law references  

These services operate independently of JusticeLens. When invoked, the GPT may send limited, non-identifying search queries (e.g., “Georgia habitual offender same occasion”) to retrieve relevant public data.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy:
- [CourtListener Privacy Policy](https://www.courtlistener.com/privacy-policy/)  
- [govinfo Privacy Policy](https://www.govinfo.gov/about/policies)  

---

## 4. Data Security

JusticeLens does not maintain servers or databases outside of OpenAI’s infrastructure. OpenAI provides security controls for data transmission, encryption, and storage. See [OpenAI’s Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/privacy) for details.

When users share sensitive legal materials, they are encouraged to **remove or redact personal identifiers** before upload.

---

## 5. Children’s Privacy

JusticeLens is not intended for use by individuals under 18 years of age. It is designed for use by adults, attorneys, law students, and legal aid organizations.

---

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

You may request that OpenAI delete your conversations or files per their account controls. JusticeLens does not retain any records independently.

If you wish to correct, remove, or inquire about information you’ve shared within a chat, you may contact OpenAI directly or email **michael.lyons@yimpactventures.com**.
